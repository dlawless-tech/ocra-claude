Findings

  1. Jan-Aug Grubhub fees under-booked by 22,283.39. Fees, marketing, promotions and refunds were 73,230.20; the Grubhub fee entries booked 50,946.81. September ties, so the whole gap is in the Jan-Aug monthly entries. Oak Park had none (14,648.21). Loop (4,271.58) and Northwestern (4,065.89) are short mainly on their catering stores (Loop GO, Northwestern catering). Every other store is within about 125, apart from Lakeview's August entry with no Grubhub behind it (finding 5).
  2. The 8/31 GrubHub AJE is not a deposit reclass. Jan-Jul Grubhub deposits posted to FARE Holding. The AJE credited each store about its full 8/31 balance (133,785.29 total), which pushed unbooked fees into Holding. Holding 1103 carries 28,283.94 that belongs to no deposit; 17,957.85 of it came from the Oak Park credit.
  3. September weekly fee entries tie. Entries are booked for every store from 9/6 through 9/27, and for 9/1-9/21 they match Grubhub within 29.38 in total (tax on refunds booked in 7535).
  4. Tips are left out of the DSS clearing debit. Grubhub paid 5,080.38 of tips in the payouts, but the DSS debits 1103 without them, so deposits over-clear the account. Mostly Loop (2,786.02) and Northwestern (1,979.40) catering and self-delivery orders.
  5. Lakeview shows Grubhub sales with no Grubhub store. DSS posted 3,024.37 of Grubhub sales to Lakeview from 8/1 to 9/21, but Lakeview is not in the Grubhub download and has no Grubhub deposits. A 463.86 fee entry was booked in August with no statement behind it.
  6. Logan Square 3/4 DSS spike. DSS posted about 2,250.66 to 1103 on 3/4 against 140.80 of Grubhub sales. No Grubhub order matches it on any date.
  7. Old Town DSS runs about 20% below Grubhub. Every day since August the DSS amount is about 20% under the Grubhub sales ((844.38) net), which suggests the Toast in-store price is posting where the Grubhub price is higher.
  8. Deposits. Every Grubhub payout through 9/25 now ties exactly to an R365 bank deposit. Deposit BD002858 (469.85, Northwestern, 7/17) matches no Grubhub payout.
  9. Catering timing. Catering orders post to DSS on the event date while Grubhub dates them when placed, so Loop and Northwestern swing day to day. These net out over the period and need no entry.
